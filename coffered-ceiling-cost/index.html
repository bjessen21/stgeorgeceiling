How Much Does a Coffered Ceiling Cost in St. George, UT?

Real price ranges by room size, material, and ceiling height — and what actually moves the number up or down.

Short Answer

$4,500 to $35,000+, Depending on Scope

A single accent room with a painted MDF grid starts around $4,500. A great room with a mid-range stained wood coffered ceiling typically runs $9,000 to $18,000. Large, high-ceiling great rooms with premium hand-finished millwork run $18,000 to $35,000 or more, and whole-home multi-room packages start above $35,000.

Those ranges hold up across most St. George and Washington County installs, but the exact number for your room depends on a handful of specific factors below — which is why we give a written, itemized quote after seeing the actual space rather than a number over the phone.

What Changes the Price

Six Factors That Move Your Number

Room Size

More square footage means more beams, more moulding, and more labor hours — the single biggest driver of total cost.

Ceiling Height

Taller ceilings (11–12+ ft) need deeper beam profiles to read correctly from the floor, which uses more material per panel.

Material

Painted MDF costs less than solid wood; high-density polyurethane sits in between and holds up well to St. George's dry heat.

Grid Complexity

A simple 3x3 grid costs less than an intricate layout with varied panel sizes or a stepped, multi-level design.

Finish

A sprayed paint finish is faster and less expensive than hand-applied stain, which takes more time to get an even, furniture-grade result.

Integrated Lighting

Recessed or accent lighting built into the panels adds electrical work on top of the carpentry, raising both material and labor cost.

Coffered vs. Beams

Is a Full Coffered Ceiling More Expensive Than Exposed Beams?

Usually, yes. A full coffered grid uses considerably more millwork — moulding around every recessed panel, not just the beams themselves — so it costs more than a simpler exposed-beam or box-grid layout in the same room. If budget is the main constraint, beam or box-grid treatments deliver a lot of the same architectural impact for less.

Resale Value

Does It Add Value to a St. George Home?

In luxury and semi-custom markets like St. George's, a coffered ceiling reads as intentional, high-end finish work rather than a cosmetic add-on — which is why it shows up more often in higher-end new construction and remodels here than in most markets. Actual resale impact still depends on your specific neighborhood and price point, so treat it as a quality-of-living upgrade first and a value add second.

Common Questions

Cost FAQ

What is the average cost of a coffered ceiling in St. George, UT?

Most St. George great-room coffered ceilings run $9,000 to $18,000 for a mid-range stained wood grid. Smaller accent rooms with painted MDF start around $4,500, and premium hand-finished installs in large great rooms with 12+ foot ceilings run $18,000 to $35,000 or more.

What drives coffered ceiling cost up or down?

The biggest factors are room size, ceiling height, material choice, grid complexity, finish, and whether you're adding integrated lighting — all covered above.

Is a coffered ceiling cheaper than exposed beams?

Generally yes for an equivalent room, since a full coffered grid uses more millwork than a simpler beam or box-grid layout.

Does a coffered ceiling add resale value to a St. George home?

It's widely viewed as a value-adding architectural upgrade in luxury and semi-custom home markets like St. George's. Actual resale impact varies by neighborhood and home price point.
