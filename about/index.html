About St. George Coffered Ceilings

Our Focus

One Thing, Done Right

St. George Coffered Ceilings exists because a coffered or beamed ceiling is a specialty, not a side job. Getting it right means sizing the grid to the room, choosing materials that hold up to Southern Utah's dry heat and temperature swings, and finishing the trim to a standard that reads as original architecture — not an add-on.

We keep our focus narrow on purpose: coffered ceiling installation, beam and box ceiling design, and the restoration work that keeps an existing ceiling looking custom. That focus means fewer surprises, straighter answers about material and cost, and a finished ceiling built for the room it's actually going in.
