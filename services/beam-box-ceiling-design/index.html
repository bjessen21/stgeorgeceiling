Beam & Box Ceiling Design in St. George, UT

Exposed beam and box-grid treatments for great rooms, kitchens, and home theaters.

Architectural Beams

Structure Without the Full Coffered Grid

Not every room needs a full coffered ceiling to feel finished. Exposed beams or a simpler box-grid pattern can add the same kind of architectural weight to a great room, kitchen, or home theater at a lower cost and with less visual density — a good fit for rooms where you want structure and character without the ceiling becoming the main event.

We design the beam layout and spacing to your room's proportions, then build it out in either faux (lightweight, hollow) or solid timber beams depending on your ceiling framing and budget, finished in stain, paint, or a reclaimed-wood look.

  • Exposed beam layouts for great rooms, kitchens, and home theaters
  • Box-grid ceiling treatments as a lighter alternative to full coffering
  • Faux or structural beam options depending on your ceiling framing
  • Stain, paint, or reclaimed-wood finish choices
  • Integrated lighting coordination on request
  • Written, itemized quote before work begins

Beam Style Options

Faux Wood Beams

Lightweight, hollow-core beams that mount to existing ceilings without structural changes — the most common option for retrofits.

Solid Timber Beams

Real structural or decorative timber for homes that want authentic weight and grain — best suited to new construction or open framing.

Box-Grid Millwork

A lighter, flatter grid pattern that adds definition to a ceiling without the depth of full beams — a good middle ground on cost.
