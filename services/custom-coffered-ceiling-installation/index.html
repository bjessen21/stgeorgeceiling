Custom Coffered Ceiling Installation in St. George, UT

Full-room coffered grids designed and installed to match your home's scale, trim style, and finish.

New Installation

A Coffered Grid Built to Your Exact Room

A custom coffered ceiling is built from a grid of beams and moulding that creates a series of recessed panels overhead. We start with an in-home measurement of your ceiling height, span, and existing trim, then design a grid layout sized specifically to that room — not a stock kit forced to fit.

From there we handle the full install: framing the grid, installing trim and moulding, finishing in paint or stain, and adding integrated recessed or accent lighting if you want it. Materials are selected for Southern Utah's dry climate, so the finished ceiling holds its lines instead of cracking at the seams a year or two in.

  • Free in-home design consultation and measurement
  • Custom grid layout sized to your exact room and ceiling height
  • Choice of solid wood, painted MDF, or high-density polyurethane millwork
  • Framing, trim, and finish work handled start to finish
  • Optional integrated recessed or accent lighting
  • Written, itemized quote before work begins

Material Options We Install

Solid Wood

Kiln-dried and properly sealed for St. George's low humidity and heat swings. The premium option for stained, natural-wood-grain finishes.

Painted MDF

A cost-effective, dimensionally stable option for painted finishes — holds crisp lines well and is a common choice for accent rooms and smaller budgets.

High-Density Polyurethane

Won't crack or warp in dry desert heat, primed and ready to paint. A strong option for larger grids where long-term stability matters most.
