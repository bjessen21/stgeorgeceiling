When a Coffered or Beam Ceiling Needs Attention

Southern Utah's dry heat and big day-to-night temperature swings are hard on millwork that wasn't sealed or installed correctly the first time — seams separate, trim cracks at the joints, and sun exposure through large windows fades a stain finish unevenly over a few years. A dated stain color or paint finish can also make an otherwise well-built ceiling feel older than the rest of a remodeled room.

We start with a free on-site assessment of the existing trim, framing, and finish condition, then scope the repair or refinish work — from spot-fixing cracked seams to a full strip-and-refinish or style update that brings an older ceiling in line with the rest of your home.

  • Free on-site assessment of existing trim and framing condition
  • Repair of cracked, separated, or water-damaged millwork
  • Refinishing with new stain, paint, or clear coat
  • Style updates to modernize a dated coffered or beam ceiling
  • Matching existing trim profile for partial-room repairs
  • Written, itemized quote before work begins

Common Issues

What We Fix Most Often

Cracked or Separated Seams

Millwork that wasn't properly sealed against St. George's temperature swings tends to crack or pull apart at the joints over time.

Sun-Faded Finish

Rooms with large west- or south-facing windows often see uneven fading on stained wood ceilings after a few years of direct sun.

Dated Stain or Paint Color

A ceiling built years ago in a color that no longer matches the rest of a remodeled room — a common request during a broader home update.
