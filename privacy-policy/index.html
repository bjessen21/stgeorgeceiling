Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information St. George Coffered Ceilings ("we," "us," or "our") collects through stgeorgeceiling.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily submit through our quote request form, including your name, phone number, email address (optional), property address or ZIP code, the service you're interested in, and any project details you choose to share.

We also collect standard technical information automatically when you visit the Site, such as your browser type, device type, and pages visited, typically through analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request and follow up about your project
  • To schedule and prepare for in-home consultations
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share the information you submit with other companies for their own marketing purposes.

How We Store Information

Quote requests submitted through this Site are stored in a private, access-controlled system used to manage inquiries and respond to you.

Cookies & Analytics

This Site may use cookies or similar technologies to understand site traffic and improve the browsing experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You can ask us to delete the information you've submitted, or to stop contacting you, at any time by calling 435-334-2921 or emailing us using the contact information on our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via the Contact page or by calling 435-334-2921.
